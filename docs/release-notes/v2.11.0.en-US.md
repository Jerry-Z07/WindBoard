# Release Notes (Desktop v2.11.0)

- ✨ feat: [Desktop & Web] Dock layout can now be edited in place on the board: drag to reorder, hide/restore individual items, add, edit, or remove shortcut entries, and auto-hide a group's panel and separators when it has no visible items. The Settings page keeps "Edit Dock" and "Restore default layout".
- ✨ feat: [Desktop] Added horizontal wheel and touchpad two-finger scrolling to pan the canvas.
- ✨ feat: [Desktop] Redesigned the crash dialog with a persistent summary/report two-pane layout and fixed layout breakage under high DPI scaling.
- 🐛 fix: [Desktop] Fixed a 1px white line at the top of the screen when the app is full screen.
- 🐛 fix: [Desktop] Fixed a white flash when entering screen annotation.
- 🐛 fix: [Desktop & Web] Fixed the page management panel content being left-aligned; thumbnails now fill the panel width.
- 🐛 fix: [Desktop & Web] Page deletion and canvas clearing now ask for confirmation. Busy dialogs for export/import can be canceled, and canceling or failing no longer leaves partial files at the chosen location. The export dialog now validates page range and margins as you type.
- 🐛 fix: [Desktop & Web] Added keyboard paths for page management, canvas clearing, and Dock settings, along with empty-state and busy feedback.
